Our Dive Center

Where Every Dive Begins

Casa Escondida's PADI-certified dive center sits steps from your room. Our experienced divemasters and instructors know every reef, crevice, and critter in Anilao — so whether you're logging your first dive or your thousandth, you're in expert hands.

Hot showers, full equipment rental, camera rinse stations, nitrox fills, and a private jetty for direct access to Anilao's legendary dive sites make every morning effortless.

Before You Dive

Dive Package Conditions

Package Inclusions
  • Dive package rates include boat fee, service of 1 Divemaster, and tank for each dive.
  • Gear is not included — rental available separately at the dive center.
  • Requests for an additional Dive Master will be charged extra to the requesting guest or group.
Dive Times
  • Average dive time is 45 minutes per dive.
  • Maximum dive time is 60 minutes.
  • The 2 Dives Out Package is limited to 2 boat dives per trip.
Cancellations & Missed Dives
  • Missed and cancelled dives are non-transferrable and non-refundable.
  • Boat and Divemaster cancellations will be charged 50% of the corresponding fee.
Gear & Equipment
  • All rented gear must be returned in proper working condition.
  • Any damage caused by the guest will be charged accordingly.
