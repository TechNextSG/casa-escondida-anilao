International Clean Up Dive 2026: Trash In, Treasure Out

September 22, 2026 · 4 min read · Casa Escondida

← Back to all posts

On 19 September 2026, divers and friends gathered at Casa Escondida Anilao Resort & Dive Center in Brgy. Ligaya, Mabini, Batangas for our International Clean Up Dive — a morning spent giving back to the reefs we love. Trash in, treasure out: every bag of debris we brought up is a small win for the marine life that makes Anilao special.

A morning for the ocean

The day began early, with a dive briefing at 8:00 AM before teams suited up and headed out. Instead of hunting for frogfish and nudibranchs, this dive had a different mission: fanning out across the site to collect the marine debris that drifts in and snags on the reef — then bringing it up for proper sorting and disposal back on shore.

It was equal parts hard work and good fun, with our dive team, guests, and visiting divers all pitching in side by side.

Why a clean-up dive matters here

Anilao sits within the Verde Island Passage, one of the most biodiverse stretches of ocean on Earth. Marine debris doesn't just look out of place — it smothers coral, entangles marine life, and breaks down into microplastics that work their way through the food chain. Removing it, piece by piece, helps keep these reefs healthy for the macro critters, turtles, and fish that divers travel across the world to see.

Every dive counts: even outside organised clean-ups, our divers follow a simple habit — if you can safely remove a piece of trash on any dive, take it up with you. Small actions, multiplied across every diver, add up.

Thank you for diving in

A huge thank you to everyone who suited up and dove in — our staff, our guests, and every diver and friend who joined us for a cleaner, healthier ocean. Days like this are a reminder that protecting Anilao's waters is a team effort, and we're grateful to share it with a community that cares.

Want to join the next one?

We host reef clean-ups and dive gatherings throughout the year. To hear about the next one — or to plan your own dive trip to Anilao — follow us on Facebook and Instagram, or book your stay with us.
